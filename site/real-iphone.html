Test on a real iPhone with a free Apple ID

Last updated: 16 November 2026

AI-OS drives real iPhones as well as simulators. It signs the iPhone driver with your own Apple ID, so you need no paid Apple Developer account and no provisioning profile made by hand.

Before you start

  • Install AI-OS and finish its setup on your Mac.
  • Connect the iPhone to the Mac with a cable once, and tap Trust.
  • Turn on Developer Mode on the iPhone: Settings, Privacy & Security.
  • Add your Apple ID in Xcode: Settings, Accounts. A free one works.

Set up the iPhone

Run this in Terminal:

~/ai-os/bin/aios phone-setup

It finds the iPhone and your signing team, builds the driver for that phone (a few minutes), starts it, and says when the signing ends. The first time, trust your developer app on the iPhone (Settings, General, VPN & Device Management), then start the driver:

~/ai-os/bin/aios -d phone phone-start

The device page of the AI-OS app shows the iPhone, and can start its driver too.

Install your app

AI-OS does not install apps on a real iPhone. Install yours there yourself, with Xcode, TestFlight or your company's app store. Then ask your agent to open it by its bundle id.

Good to know

  • A free Apple ID signs for 7 days. Run phone-setup again to sign again; the setup check shows the days left.
  • The iPhone must be unlocked and on the same Wi-Fi as the Mac, or on a cable, with any VPN on the Mac turned off.
  • Expect about 1.4 s per tap step over Wi-Fi.
  • Some apps crash when their whole screen is read, for example Flutter apps built before Flutter 3.47.
  • Turn on Do Not Disturb before you capture: banners from other apps show in screenshots.
  • Screen video on an iPhone needs ffmpeg. Setup offers to install it.

Safety

While the driver runs, others on the same Wi-Fi could reach it. Use a network you trust, and stop the driver when you are done:

~/ai-os/bin/aios -d phone phone-stop

To lock the driver, set phone_driver_token to true in ~/.config/ai-os/config.json and run phone-setup once. The driver then gets a new random token each time it starts, so only AI-OS can use it.

Other tools

Of the tools we compared on 3 November 2026, Maestro and MobileBuildMCP do not drive real iPhones, and Mobile MCP and Appium MCP need a provisioning profile from you. See how AI-OS compares.

Download for Mac

Free to use. For Macs with Apple silicon, macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later, and Xcode 26 or later.
