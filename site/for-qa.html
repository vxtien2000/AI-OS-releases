iOS testing with AI, for QA and testers

Last updated: 16 November 2026

You do not write test code. You describe the test to your AI agent in plain words; the agent turns it into steps, and AI-OS runs them on a simulator or a real iPhone and checks each one.

How a test goes

  1. Get a simulator build from your developers or your CI: a .app, or a .zip holding one. Put it in the builds folder of the AI-OS app (Settings, Logins).
  2. Save the test account's password in Logins, under a name such as APP_PASSWORD.
  3. Ask your agent, in Claude Code, Claude Desktop or Codex. For example:

Use AI-OS: install MyApp-staging.zip clean on an iPhone simulator, log in with the password saved as APP_PASSWORD, add a product to the cart and check that the cart shows 1 item.

Watch the run live in the AI-OS app. When the AI is stuck, on a captcha or a login code, take control: its test pauses, and when you hand back it goes on from the same step.

What you get

  • A pass or a fail for every step. A check passes only on the exact words: “Saved” does not match “Unsaved changes”.
  • The run history in the AI-OS app: the device, the app, every step, and the question or error. Passwords are removed.
  • Screenshots and screen video, when you or the agent ask for them.
  • Notes the agent keeps about each app, so the next run starts with what the last one learned.

Made for testing

  • Passwords stay out of the chat. The AI asks for one by name and AI-OS types it, only into a password box of an app you allow.
  • Questions, not hangs. When something unexpected shows up, AI-OS asks the agent; one rule answers it every time after.
  • Several simulators at once. Each AI session keeps its own device, so two never tap in each other's way.
  • Device prep per app on simulators: permissions, appearance and location, set before the app first opens.
  • Team labels for your builds, for example STG for staging.
  • The AI-OS app works without an AI agent too: turn devices on, take screenshots, record.
  • No telemetry. AI-OS runs on your Mac.
