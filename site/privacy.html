AI-OS privacy notice

Last updated: 2 November 2026

AI-OS is a tool that runs on your Mac and lets AI agents and people test iOS apps on simulators and iPhones. This notice explains what data AI-OS handles, where it stays, and what leaves your Mac.

In short: AI-OS has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. Everything it stores stays on your Mac. AI-OS itself never sends your data to an AI model; your own AI agent does that, under its provider’s terms.

Who we are

AI-OS is made by Bruce Vu. Contact: vxtien@lolconsultants.com.

What AI-OS stores, and where

All of it is on your Mac, readable only by your macOS user:

WhatWhere
Settings (device names, folders, your signing team for a real iPhone)~/.config/ai-os/config.json
Passwords you choose to add, for test accounts~/.config/ai-os/secrets.env (AI-OS refuses to use it unless only you can read it)
Notes your AI agents keep about each app~/.config/ai-os/notes/
Screenshots, videos and run records~/Desktop/ai-os-captures/ (you can change this)
Working state and logs~/.cache/ai-os/, ~/Library/Application Support/ai-os/
When the app last checked for updatesThe app’s preferences (local.ai-os.app)

Screenshots and videos can contain whatever the app under test shows. Use test accounts and test data.

Remove everything with ~/ai-os/uninstall.sh (it asks before removing your settings, passwords and captures).

Passwords

  • Your AI agent never sees a password. It asks AI-OS to type one by its name (for example TEST_USER_PW).
  • AI-OS types a password only into a password field of an app you allowed, never into a web page, and never while the screen is being recorded. It takes no screenshot for 2 seconds afterwards.
  • Everything AI-OS returns or writes (replies to your agent, terminal output, run records) is checked, and any password value is replaced with [secret].

AI processing

AI-OS does not contain or call any AI model. When you connect an AI agent (for example Claude Code, Claude Desktop or Codex), that agent reads screen text and screenshots from AI-OS and sends them to its AI provider, under the provider’s own terms. Check those terms before testing confidential apps:

  • Anthropic (Claude Code): data usage and privacy policy
  • OpenAI (Codex): your data and privacy policy

Business and API plans of these providers usually have stricter data terms than personal plans.

The website

This site is served by Cloudflare. To run and protect it, Cloudflare keeps the site’s request logs for 3 days (the request, the response and related metadata, which may include your IP address). The site’s pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser also contacts Google, under Google’s privacy policy. The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

To learn whether the page is useful, the site keeps a few counts that do not identify anyone:

WhenWhat is counted
You open the home pageThe site that linked to it, if another site did (its name only, for example github.com, never the page or search on it), and your country
You download AI-OS through the site’s /download linkWhich of the two Download for Mac buttons you used, if any, your country, and the site the link was on (its name only)
You leave the home pageHow many seconds you spent on it (time in a hidden tab does not count), how far you scrolled (as a percentage), which sections came into view and which tabs you opened

Your country comes from Cloudflare. These counts carry no IP address, no cookie and no identifier, so they cannot be linked to you or to one another. They are kept by Cloudflare (Workers Analytics Engine) for 3 months, then deleted.

If your browser asks sites not to track you (Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track), the page sends nothing at all. The site uses no third-party analytics, advertising, heatmaps or session recording.

Your choices and rights

Because AI-OS keeps your data on your Mac, you can see, change or delete it at any time in the folders above. For questions about data on the website or in support emails, write to vxtien@lolconsultants.com. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Support emails

If you email us, we keep your message and address to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed to answer you. Please do not send passwords or customer data; run.json and doctor output already have passwords removed.

Changes

We will update this notice when AI-OS’s data handling changes, and note the change in the release notes.
